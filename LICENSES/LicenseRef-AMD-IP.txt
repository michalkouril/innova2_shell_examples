LicenseRef-AMD-IP

The bitstreams, flash images, xclbin/xsabin files and platform archives in this repository were produced by AMD
Vivado/Vitis. Besides the design of the innova2 contributors (Apache-2.0), they contain AMD (Xilinx) IP cores
generated by those tools (for example XDMA/PCIe, DDR4 MIG, SmartConnect, HWICAP, clocking). Those cores are
provided under the terms of the AMD tool and IP licences that applied when they were generated, not under
Apache-2.0. Redistribution of the binaries is subject to those terms.

Vitis platform archives (hw.xsa) likewise carry Vivado-generated output of the stock AMD IP in the design, which
v++ needs to link kernels; that output is under AMD's terms too.

History: the earlier release rbdm r1 (2026-09-28) also contained the netlist of pf_demux from AMD's U2 platform
package. inn2x r3 (2026-09-29) and later use the innova2 contributors' own pf_demux (Apache-2.0).
